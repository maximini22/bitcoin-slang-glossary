Robbie P is an Australian artist, lyricist, and producer who turns sound money, finance, and the political zeitgeist into street-level hip-hop — often called your favorite hedge fund’s favorite rapper. The catalog runs from early Bitcoin singles through Freedom and Dread Pirate Robbie. He has appeared on Pleb Underground, Bullish Bit with BTC Sessions, and with Coach Carbon, and performed at Camp Nakamoto and Bitcoin Alive. Music lives on robbiep.net; this site is the lyric glossary for bitcoinslang.com.
